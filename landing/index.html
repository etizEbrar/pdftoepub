The difference

A PDF describes a page. An ebook has to describe a book.

A PDF knows where every glyph sits and nothing about what any of it means. Every defect below is one we measured in a real Turkish edited volume and then fixed.

  • Chapter titles cut in half A title set over three display lines contributes only the line that happened to score as a heading.

How it works

Four stages, and only one of them is a model.

Each stage runs where it is cheapest and most private. The expensive, non-deterministic option is reached for last and least.

  1. Read the scan on your iPhone

    Pages without a text layer go to Apple Vision at its .accurate level, which reads Turkish natively. The page images never leave the device — only the recognised words and their positions are uploaded.

    on device · Apple Vision

  2. Rebuild the typesetting

    Lines become paragraphs, hyphens broken at the margin are resolved against the document's own vocabulary, ligatures and Turkish glyphs are restored, and headers, footers and page numbers are dropped. Deterministic: the same book in, the same book out, every time.

    server · deterministic

  3. Ask Claude about what is genuinely ambiguous

    Some calls cannot be made from geometry: on a scanned page, type size carries almost no signal, and a bold centred line may be a chapter title, a pull-quote or a caption. Only the fragments scored below the confidence threshold are sent to Claude, and only to be classified.

    optional · Claude Haiku 4.5

  4. Build and validate the EPUB

    One cleanly slugified XHTML document per chapter, a trimmed navigation tree, an EPUB2 NCX for Kindle, and the whole archive checked by EPUBCheck before you ever see it.

    server · EPUBCheck 5.3.0

Claude is the second opinion on structure — never the editor of your prose.

The deterministic engine resolves the overwhelming majority of a book on its own. What it cannot always settle is what a fragment is: whether a short bold centred line opens a chapter, labels a table, or is a line of verse. Geometry answers that well in a typeset PDF and poorly in a scan. For exactly those fragments, Claude is asked to classify — and the request is bounded on purpose.

  • Only the uncertain fragments Blocks the deterministic pass scored below its confidence threshold. A clean book may send none at all.
  • Never the whole document Each request carries the fragment and the lines either side of it, every field capped at a few hundred characters. Never a page, never the book, never the PDF itself.
  • Classification, not editing The reply is a structural role and a heading level. The system prompt forbids rewriting, correcting, translating, completing or summarising any text.
  • A failure costs nothing If the call errors or returns something unexpected, the deterministic answer stands and the conversion finishes.

Cloud review is opt-in and off by default. With it disabled the app converts entirely with local, deterministic processing — including OCR, tables, formulas and right-to-left text — and requires no API key and no paid service of any kind.

Features

What you get in the finished book.

On-device neural OCR

Apple Vision reads scanned pages on the Neural Engine at about two seconds a page, with Turkish supported natively. Private by construction: the page images stay on your phone.

Measured: 2.1 s/page vs 110 s server-side

Claude for ambiguous structure

A bounded second opinion on the fragments geometry cannot settle — a fragment and its neighbours at a time, classification only, with the deterministic result as the fallback.

Opt-in · claude-haiku-4-5

Privacy by design

No accounts, no analytics, no tracking. Your PDF is uploaded only to build your EPUB and is deleted as soon as the book reaches your device. Documents are never used to train anything.

Deleted after conversion

Standards-compliant EPUB3

Reflowable, semantic XHTML with one document per chapter, a trimmed navigation tree and an EPUB2 NCX beside it, so Apple Books, Kobo and Kindle all read it properly.

Checked with EPUBCheck before delivery

Tables, formulas and verse kept

Real tables become real tables, formulas become MathML, and a poem keeps the line breaks the poet wrote. Anything the engine cannot reconstruct safely is preserved as an image of the original.

A picture of the right table beats a wrong one

Turkish typography, properly

Dotted and dotless i, ş, ğ, ç, ö, ü survive extraction, OCR and case-folding intact — including the dotted-capital trap that turns "İçin" into three codepoints that match nothing.

Turkish-first, not Turkish-tolerated
